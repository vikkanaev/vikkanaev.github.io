**TG-Digest** is a news summarization app. It collects posts from the Telegram news channels you follow and turns them into a short, readable digest, so you can catch up on what matters in a few minutes instead of scrolling through hundreds of messages.

## What it does

- Gathers news from your selected Telegram channels.
- Summarizes the posts into a concise digest of the key stories.
- Saves digests to your Google account (Google Drive, Docs and Sheets) so you can read, search and keep them.

## Google account access

TG-Digest asks you to sign in with Google and grant access to Google Drive, Docs and Sheets. This access is used only to create and update your digest documents and spreadsheets. Your data is not used for advertising and is not sold to anyone. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) for details.
